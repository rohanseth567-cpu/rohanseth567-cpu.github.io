This privacy policy applies to the Aqua Flow application (hereby referred to as "Application") for Android mobile devices, created by Rohan Seth (hereby referred to as "Service Provider"). The Application is provided as a free service and is intended for use "AS IS".

The Service Provider takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information the Application does and does not collect.

Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you.

Specifically, the Application does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact details
  • Your location
  • Your photos, contacts, or files
  • Any account or login information

The Application does not require you to create an account or sign in to play.

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data.

Game Data Stored on Your Device

The Application saves your game data locally on your device only. This includes information such as your puzzle progress, stars earned, in-game coins, and settings.

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to the Service Provider or to any third party, and it is not stored on any server. If you uninstall the Application or clear its data from your device settings, this information is permanently removed.

Third-Party Services

The Application is distributed through the Google Play Store, which is operated by Google. Google may collect certain information in connection with your download and use of applications from the Play Store, in accordance with its own policies. Please refer to the Google Privacy Policy for details.

The Application itself does not integrate advertising networks, analytics services, or cloud storage services.

Permissions

The Application does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or any other sensitive device permission.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because all game data is stored only on your device, you have full control over it. To delete all data associated with the Application, simply uninstall it, or clear the app's data from your device settings (Settings → Apps → Aqua Flow → Storage → Clear Data).

The Service Provider does not hold any of your data and therefore has nothing to delete on your behalf. If you have any questions about this, you may contact the Service Provider at the email address below.

Security

Because the Application does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal data held by the Service Provider that could be exposed. Game data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security measures.

Children's Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions or concerns, please contact the Service Provider at the email address below.

Your Privacy Rights
