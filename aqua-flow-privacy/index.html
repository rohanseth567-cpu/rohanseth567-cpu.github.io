Privacy Policy for Aqua Flow

Effective date: 11 July 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Aqua Flow ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you play our mobile game. Aqua Flow is published by Rohan Seth. By downloading or playing the Game, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Aqua Flow does not require you to create an account, and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number in order to play. However, the Game and the third-party services it uses may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device and technical information — such as device model, operating system version, language, region, and unique device identifiers.
  • Advertising identifier — such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID), used to serve and measure advertisements.
  • Usage and gameplay data — such as levels played, progress, in-game events, session length, crashes, and performance data, used to understand how the Game is used and to improve it.
  • Purchase information — records of in-app purchases you make (see Section 5). Payment card details are processed by Google and are never collected or stored by us.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Game and its features.
  • Save and restore your game progress on your device.
  • Display advertisements, including personalized advertisements where permitted.
  • Process and deliver in-app purchases.
  • Analyze usage trends, diagnose technical problems, and fix bugs.
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, and violations of our terms.

3. Third-Party Services

Aqua Flow uses trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data. The services we use include:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements.
  • Google Firebase / Google Analytics — for analytics and crash reporting.
  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing — for core app functionality and processing in-app purchases.
  • Google Play Games Services — where used, for features such as leaderboards and achievements.

You can review how Google collects and uses data in the Google Privacy Policy. For advertising specifically, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Advertising

The Game displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. These ads may be personalized based on your advertising identifier and use of the Game and other apps. Where required by law, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier at any time through your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads). You may also opt out of personalized advertising while continuing to see non-personalized ads.

5. In-App Purchases

Aqua Flow offers optional in-app purchases, such as coin packs and bundles. All payments are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive, collect, or store your payment card number or billing details. We may receive confirmation that a purchase was completed (for example, the product purchased and a transaction identifier) in order to deliver the item to your game. Purchases are subject to the Google Play Terms of Service.

6. Children's Privacy

Aqua Flow is a casual puzzle game suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a manner that violates applicable law. Where the Game is directed to or accessible by children, advertising and data collection are configured to comply with applicable requirements, including the Google Play Families Policy. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your game progress is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the Game or clearing the app's data will remove locally stored progress. Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their respective policies.

You may request deletion of any data associated with you that we control by contacting us at the email address below. Please include enough information for us to identify the relevant data. We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the Game. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Your continued use of the Game after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
