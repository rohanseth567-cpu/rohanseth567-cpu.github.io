This privacy policy applies to the Aqua Flow application (hereby referred to as "Application") for Android mobile devices, created by Rohan Seth (hereby referred to as "Service Provider"). The Application is provided as a free-to-play, ad-supported service with optional in-app purchases, intended for use "AS IS".

The Service Provider takes your privacy seriously. This policy is designed to help you understand what data is collected, how it is used and stored, and what choices you have.

Information Collection and Use

The Application itself does not directly ask you for personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. However, the Application and the third-party services it uses may collect and store certain data, including your game progress and purchases, which are saved to your account so they can be restored across devices. The Application does not collect precise location data, personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other directly identifiable personal information beyond the account identifiers described above.

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data.

Account and Sign-In

Aqua Flow uses Google Play Games sign-in to identify you and to link your saved game data to your account. When you sign in, the Application receives an account identifier (via Google Play Games Services and Firebase Authentication) that is used solely to save and restore your progress, currency, items, and purchases. The Application does not receive your Google account password. Signing in also enables leaderboards and achievements where those features are offered.

If you choose not to sign in, you can still play the Application; in that case your progress is stored only on your device and will not be backed up or restorable across devices.

In-App Purchases

Aqua Flow offers optional in-app purchases, such as coin packs and bundles. All payments are processed securely by Google Play Billing. The Service Provider does not receive, collect, or store your payment card number or billing details at any time.

To deliver purchased items and to support restoring purchases, the Service Provider stores a record of completed purchases (such as the product purchased, a transaction identifier, and your account identifier) on secure cloud servers (see "Cloud Storage & Data Backup" below). Purchases are subject to the Google Play Terms of Service.

Cloud Storage & Data Backup

When you are signed in, the Application saves your game data to secure cloud servers operated through Google Firebase so that it can be backed up and restored across devices and reinstalls. The data stored in your account may include:

  • Your account identifier (from Google Play Games sign-in)
  • Your game progress — levels completed, stars earned, and current level
  • Your in-game currency (coins) and items (power counts, lives)
  • Your in-app purchase history and entitlements

This data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored securely. It is used solely to operate the Application's save, restore, and purchase features. The Service Provider does not sell, trade, or rent this data to third parties.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling it using the standard uninstall processes available on your mobile device or via the Google Play Store.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device's ad settings: go to Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization on your Android device.

You can choose not to sign in with Google Play Games, or sign out at any time through your device settings, in which case your data will not be backed up to the cloud. To request deletion of cloud-stored data, see "Data Retention and Deletion" below.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data cached on your device is removed when you uninstall the Application or clear the app's data from your device settings.

When you are signed in, your cloud data stored via Firebase — including your account identifier, game progress, currency, items, in-app purchase history, and entitlements — is retained so that you can restore it across devices. This data is kept until you request its deletion.

To request deletion of your cloud-stored data, please visit our Data Deletion page or email the Service Provider at the address below. Verified requests will be processed within a reasonable timeframe.
