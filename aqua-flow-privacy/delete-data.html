Data Deletion Request

Last Updated: July 25, 2026

This page explains how you can request deletion of data associated with your use of the Aqua Flow application, created by Rohan Seth ("Service Provider").

What Data Can Be Deleted

Upon a verified request, the Service Provider will delete the data stored in association with your account on our cloud servers (Google Firebase), which may include:

  • Your account identifier and the email and display name provided when you signed in
  • Your game progress — levels completed, stars earned, and current level
  • Your in-game currency (coins) and items (power counts, lives)
  • Your purchased items and any related records held by us

Local data cached on your device is removed automatically when you uninstall the Application or clear the app's data from your device settings.

How to Request Deletion

You can delete your account and data directly in the app at any time, through the account options in the Application. This removes your cloud data and account immediately.

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, you can request deletion by email:

Email: rohanseth567@gmail.com

Subject: Aqua Flow Data Deletion Request

In your email, please include enough information to identify your data (for example, the email address you signed in with). This helps the Service Provider locate and delete the correct records.

What Happens Next

  1. The Service Provider will verify the request to confirm it relates to your data.
  2. Your cloud-stored data will be permanently deleted within a reasonable timeframe after verification.
  3. You will receive a confirmation once the deletion is complete.

Important Notes

  • Deleting your data removes your saved progress, in-game currency, items, and purchased content stored with your account. If you sign in again afterwards, a new empty account is created and your previous progress cannot be recovered.
  • Some records may be retained where required by law or for legitimate purposes such as fraud prevention and financial record-keeping, as permitted by applicable regulations.
  • Purchase transaction records held by Google (as the payment processor) are subject to Google's own data policies and retention requirements.

For more information on how your data is handled, please see our Privacy Policy.
